Privacy Policy

Effective 7 October 2026 · ELAQ Limited

Altar collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app has no server and makes no network requests of its own.

What stays on your iPhone

Your reading streak, saved verses, highlights, bookmarks, memorisation decks, prayer journal and settings are stored only on your device, in Altar's private storage and an App Group shared with Altar's own widgets and Screen Time extensions. Deleting the app deletes them.

Screen Time

Prayer Lock uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) framework. The apps you choose are represented by opaque tokens that Apple provides; Altar cannot see which apps they are, how you use them, or your browsing. That information never leaves Apple's sandbox on your device.

Photos

If you save a wallpaper, Altar asks for add-only access and writes that one image. It cannot read your library.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

Altar does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Contact

Questions: hello@prayaltar.com. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
