Prayer Lock isn't blocking anything

Open Altar › Lock › Allow Screen Time, approve the Screen Time prompt, then choose apps. Check that Prayer Lock is switched on and that you are inside a lock window.

I'm locked out

Open the blocked app, tap Open Altar, and either complete the unlock rule or use Emergency unlock (available after its cooldown). You can also switch Prayer Lock off in Altar › Lock at any time.

Restore Altar+

Altar › Me › Settings › Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Cancel a subscription

Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions › Altar.
