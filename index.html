How it works

Your thumb already knows how to scroll.
Give it something better.

1

Scroll

A full-screen, vertical feed of verses and short passages, matched to the topics you pick: anxiety, hope, rest, courage, grief and eleven more. Double-tap to say Amen, save the ones that land, or open the whole chapter in context.

2

Pray first

Prayer Lock uses Apple’s Screen Time to keep the apps you choose closed. To open them, read a few verses, pray for a minute, or recite a verse from memory. Then they’re yours for 5, 15 or 30 minutes.

3

Keep the flame lit

Set a daily goal in verses or minutes. Meet it and your streak grows, along with the flame on your Me tab. Advent, Lent and 40 Days Off Doomscrolling give the habit a shape.

There is always a way out

Once the day’s unlocks are used, an emergency unlock opens your apps for 5 minutes, once an hour. Prayer Lock can be switched off in Altar at any time, and Screen Time access can be removed in Settings. This is a nudge toward prayer, not a cage.

Prayer Lock uses Apple’s Screen Time. Altar never sees which apps you choose or how you use them: Apple gives it sealed tokens, not names.

Questions

Good questions.

Is Altar really free?

Yes. The full feed and Bible reader in every translation, the verse of the day, every topic collection, your prayer journal and its full history, one Prayer Lock, streaks, the current Advent or Lent challenge, 40 Days Off Doomscrolling and Home Screen widgets are free, with no time limit and no ads. Altar+ adds more lock rules and schedules, every theme, memorise decks, challenge archives, Lock Screen widgets and streak freezes.

How does Prayer Lock work?

You give Altar permission to use Apple's Screen Time, then choose the apps that pull you in. When one of them is locked, iOS shows a “Pray first” screen with a verse. Tap Open Altar, do your unlock (read 3 verses on the free plan), and those apps open for 5, 15 or 30 minutes, whichever you chose. Then they lock again.

What if I really need an app right now?

You are never trapped. You can switch Prayer Lock off in Altar’s Lock tab at any time. If you’ve used all of the day’s unlocks (5 a day; with Altar+ you can set it anywhere from 1 to 20), an emergency unlock opens your apps for 5 minutes, once an hour. You can also remove Altar’s Screen Time access in Settings, or delete the app.

Can Altar see which apps I use?

No. Apple hands Altar sealed tokens for the apps you pick, not their names or your usage. Altar can ask iOS to lock those tokens, and that’s all. Your Screen Time data stays with Apple on your iPhone.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

Neither. There is no sign-up of any kind, and the whole Bible is built into the app, so reading, the feed, Prayer Lock, streaks and challenges all work offline. You only need a connection to buy or restore Altar+, which goes through Apple.

Which Bible translations are included?

King James Version (public domain outside the UK; in the UK, Crown copyright, administered by Cambridge University Press) and World English Bible (public domain), complete. You can switch between them at any time, search every verse, and highlight and bookmark as you read.

When do the Advent and Lent challenges start?

Advent 2026 runs from Sunday 29 November to Thursday 24 December: 26 days. Lent 2027 runs from Ash Wednesday, 10 February, through Easter Sunday, 28 March: 47 days. Every day has a reading and a short prayer prompt. Missed a day? You have 3 days to catch up. The dates are calculated in the app each year, not hard-coded. 40 Days Off Doomscrolling can start any day.

What does Altar+ cost?

$29.99 a year with a 3-day free trial, $6.99 a month, or $59.99 once for lifetime access. Those are US prices; the App Store shows the price for your country.

How do I cancel Altar+?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Altar+. Apple handles all billing, so we never see your card. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

Is there an iPad or Android version?

Not yet. Altar is for iPhone, on iOS 17 or later.
